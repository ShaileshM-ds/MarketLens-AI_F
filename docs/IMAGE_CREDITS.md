# Image credits

Demo products use openly licensed stock photos of **similar** products, stored locally in `frontend/public/demo-photos/`.
They are not photos of the fictional Demo products. The app captions each one "Stock photo of a similar product; colour and model may differ" and credits it on the product page.
Files are the 600 px versions served by Wikimedia Commons and are otherwise unchanged. Some are cropped on screen with CSS only.
Mobile phones keep their local SVG artwork, because no suitable brand-free photo was found.
